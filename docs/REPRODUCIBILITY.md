## Reproduction note RN-1 (stored-threshold rounding)

The Elevated thresholds were stored about 1e-17 below the exact calibration quantile. Seven binary and one multiclass calibration flows are tied exactly at the quantile, so they are rejected. The effective calibration coverage is therefore 0.989985 (binary) and 0.989996 (multiclass) rather than 0.990010 and 0.990000. All evaluations used the stored values, so all results are internally consistent. Table 3 of the manuscript gives the thresholds at full precision.

## Reproduction note RN-2 (CSV round-trip)

Reading thresholds back from CSV requires `pd.read_csv(..., float_precision="round_trip")`. pandas' default parser may differ in the last bit.
