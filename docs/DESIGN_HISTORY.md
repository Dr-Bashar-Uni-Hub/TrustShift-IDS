# Design history and post hoc analyses

1. **V1 foundation.** The detectors, calibration and fixed selective policy were developed in notebook 01. Selective-policy development splits the calibration partition into development and holdout halves. The original Step 9 made this split at row level, so duplicate feature-hash groups could fall on both sides. Before any distribution-shift evaluation it was replaced by a group-aware split (StratifiedGroupKFold, 2 folds, `group_key = feature_hash`; Steps 9R/10R/11R). IID-test results had been observed before the correction, but the correction was not triggered by them and the IID-test set was never used for model, ranking-method or threshold selection. IID predictive outputs were preserved and the IID selective results regenerated (`artifacts/metadata/TrustShift_IDS_STEP_9_*`, `STEP_10_*`, `STEP_11_*`).
2. **Monitor redesign.** The candidate SSS contained five evidence families, two of them based on tree-leaf familiarity (boundaries 0.4088 / 0.7695). A label-free check on the 82 IID-test windows (notebook 02, V2.4A–C) showed excess escalation. The leaf families were removed and the boundaries re-derived from the 54-window source null (0.441345 / 0.679142).
3. **Policy thresholds.** The Elevated (99%) and Severe (90%) calibration-coverage thresholds were chosen after the fixed-policy shift results were known (notebook 02, V2.10). They were then frozen.
4. **Frozen evaluation.** Notebook 02 covers V2.11–V2.14: controlled and compound shifts, deployment sequences, multi-seed robustness, and ablation.
5. **POST HOC (revision).** Notebooks 03–04 and `analysis/` add the following: static and coverage-matched static thresholds, the lag-1 policy, single-family and ATC monitors, the oracle, security metrics, McNemar tests, bootstrap intervals, and the adaptive outcome on CSE-CIC-IDS2018. These analyses changed the study's conclusion from a method claim to the evaluation reported in the manuscript.
